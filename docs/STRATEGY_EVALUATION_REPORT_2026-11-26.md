# TRADEDEN Strategy Evaluation — Research Baseline, 26 November 2026

**Bottom line: no strategy in TRADEDEN has a demonstrated edge after costs.** Of 34 strategies
(20 existing bank strategies, 8 new ICT setups, 6 audited upgrades), 33 could be evaluated and
**none passes validation**. Twelve are significantly *negative* after costs. Every Holm-adjusted
p-value is 1.000. Nothing has been promoted; nothing should be.

The few strategies with positive numbers are small, unstable across periods and instruments, and
need on the order of 1,100–1,900 further trades each before a +0.05R edge could even be detected.
The honest description is: one forward-test candidate (Opening Range Breakout v2), a handful of
watch-list strategies, and a large set that should be suspended or redesigned, including the
production trendline engine that drives the Market Radar.

Full tables: `docs/STRATEGY_EVALUATION_BASELINE_TABLES.md` (generated by `python -m research.intel_report`).
Method and strategy definitions: `docs/STRATEGY_INTELLIGENCE.md`.

**Integrity check.** The 19 pre-existing strategies with a prior backtest (`historical.json`,
generated 2026-10-19 by a separate script) were reproduced **exactly** (trades, expectancy, profit
factor, test-period expectancy: 0 mismatches). The evaluation pipeline is therefore consistent with
the earlier independent run.

**Run note.** The first full run was correct but impractically slow: the pure-Python bootstrap
(about 3 s per 5,000 trades) was invoked for every period, fold and breakdown group on one core. It
was stopped and replaced, *inside the runner only*, by an equivalent vectorised bootstrap (same
4,000 seeded percentile resamples; verified to agree within Monte-Carlo noise). Breakdowns now use
point statistics. `research/metrics.py` is unchanged for every other research ledger.

## 4. Out-of-sample, walk-forward and 2× costs

- **Out-of-sample (dev > 0 and test > 0):** only Bollinger (+0.016 / +0.077), ORB v2 (+0.053 / +0.048) and ICT Sweep + FVG (+0.104 / +0.007, 12 test trades). Everything else is negative in at least one.
- **Instability:** London Breakout is −0.024R in development and +0.083R in test, with a test-period CI above zero [+0.01, +0.15]. Per instrument, it is negative in development on 9 of 12 instruments and positive in test on 9 of 12. That is a **regime effect in 2026**, not a stable edge. Original ORB flips the other way (+0.056 → −0.132). Market Structure Break and Bollinger also flip sign per instrument between periods.
- **Walk-forward (positive folds out of 7):** ORB v2 6/7, Bollinger 5/7, ORB 5/7, Order Block Retest 5/7. Every SUSPENDED strategy is at 0–1/7 except Trend Pullback v2 (3/7) and Support & Resistance (2/7). The production trendline engines score 0/7 (Bounce) and 1/7 (Break & Retest).
- **2× costs:** only **ORB v2 (+0.020R)** and **ICT Sweep + FVG (+0.029R, n=48)** stay positive. Bollinger (−0.024R), London Breakout (−0.031R) and ORB (−0.011R) do not survive. Every small positive edge in the bank is smaller than its trading costs.
- **Significance:** Holm-adjusted p = 1.000 for every strategy. Even the best unadjusted results are consistent with chance after 33 comparisons.

## 7. Suspend or redesign

**Suspension recommended (significantly negative and currently alerting on Telegram):** EMA
Crossover, EMA Pullback, Donchian, Liquidity Sweep, Inside Bar Breakout, ATR Volatility Breakout,
Ichimoku. These are EXPERIMENTAL bank strategies with CEO-approved alerts. Each has a 95% CI
entirely below zero on 2,300–6,700 trades, and 0–1 positive walk-forward folds out of 7. **I have
not switched anything off: this is your decision.**

**Redesign: the production trendline engine (Trendline Break & Retest, Trendline Bounce) and Support
& Resistance.** These drive the Market Radar lifecycle and the production Telegram alerts. They are
the three worst strategies in the system: −0.20R, −0.23R and −0.13R per trade, with
"confirmed" win rates of 24–30% against planned R:R of 2.6–3.5. The targets (H1 structural targets)
are too far for the stops (M15 structural invalidation). A redesign should start from the stop and
target geometry, not from entry filters. Until then, a CONFIRMED radar state means "this rule's
conditions were met", not "this is a good trade". The Radar now shows evidence tiers next to the
strategies for that reason.

**Redesign or retire among the new work:** ICT Order Block Reversal (significantly negative), ICT
OTE (target geometry), Trend Pullback v2 (the upgrade is as negative as the original: the "trend
regime" hypothesis failed), Compression Breakout v2 and Trend Momentum v2 (too restrictive: 109–138
trades, negative).

## 8. Execution assumptions that could make these backtests optimistic

These matter because the surviving edges are 0.02–0.05R, smaller than one spread on most instruments.

1. **Entry spread from the bar record.** Entries fill at the next M15 open plus that bar's recorded MT5 spread, with no entry slippage. The bar spread field under-represents spreads at session opens, news and rollover. That is exactly when ORB (cash opens), London Breakout and the ICT kill-zone setups enter.
2. **Targets fill exactly at the level**; stops slip only 0.5 × spread (except at bar-open gaps, which fill at the open). News-driven stop slippage can be several spreads.
3. **Costs are one broker's.** IC Markets Raw-style commission on FX and metals; indices and crypto are spread-only. Financing uses today's swap rates applied to past trades.
4. **Live gates are not all reproduced.** Quote freshness and tick age cannot be replayed. The live signal-quality gate (`signal_quality`, enforce mode) is not applied in the backtest, so the live signal set differs from the simulated one.
5. **One position per strategy and symbol; no portfolio layer.** Results are R per trade, not a funded account. There is no correlation between simultaneous FX positions, no risk caps, no capacity or partial-fill modelling.
6. **Same-bar ambiguity** before July 2026 (no M1 data) is resolved pessimistically as a stop. This is conservative, and rare (single digits per strategy).
7. **Data reuse, the most important caveat.** The test window was already viewed in V6/T-033 for related families. The six upgrades were designed *after* reading the originals' all-period results, so their "test" numbers are not a clean hold-out. ICT v1.1's two-candle sweep rule was adopted after a frequency check on gold. **Treat every positive result here as a hypothesis for forward testing, not as out-of-sample proof.**
8. **One feed, two years, 16 instruments chosen today.** No 2020–2023 regimes for these rules.
9. **Production strategies are replayed.** The linked strategies use the research replay of the scanner (`research/lab.py`), which approximates the live engine.

## 9. Recommendations for forward testing

Nothing is promoted to production and nothing is activated on any account. The ICT family and the
upgrades are already registered as RESEARCH (shadow): once the market engine restarts they are
scanned live, recorded and outcome-tracked, never alerted. That shadow record *is* the forward test.
A demo-account execution test (like `fwd-xau-001`) would need its own protocol and your activation.

**Forward-test (shadow now; demo-account candidate after ~6 months of consistent shadow results):**
1. **Opening Range Breakout v2.** The only strategy with ≥100 trades that is positive in development and test, survives 2× costs, and is positive in 6/7 folds. Instrument pattern stable across periods (GER40/US500 positive, NAS100 negative). Pre-register *now*, before forward data arrives: GER40 + US500 as the primary hypothesis, NAS100 as a control. The edge (+0.05R) is below the precision of the evidence (CI [−0.06, +0.16]); the realistic outcome may be zero.

**Keep recording, no demo yet:**
2. **Bollinger Mean Reversion.** Positive in development and test, 5/7 folds, but does not survive 2× costs. Exclude ETHUSD (negative in both periods).
3. **ICT Sweep + Displacement + FVG.** The best ICT result and survives 2× costs, but 48 trades. Shadow recording across 16 instruments adds only ~25 trades a year at M15. Consider an M5 version through the separate M5 research extension before investing more time.
4. **London Breakout.** Watch for the 2026 regime: if forward results stay positive the regime may be persisting, if they revert it was a 2026 effect.

**Not worth further testing in current form:** all SUSPENDED strategies (§7), ICT OB Reversal, ICT
OTE, ICT FVG Continuation, Trend Pullback v2, Compression Breakout v2, Trend Momentum v2.

## 11. Safety

No execution was enabled or unlocked, no order was placed, no risk limit or credential was changed,
and nothing was switched off. The live market engine on :8000 was not restarted or interrupted. The
intelligence UI was verified through a read-only harness on :8001 that proxies the live engine. The
new strategies run only in shadow mode (never alerted).
